Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Brother Bee Industries LLC ("Brother Bee Industries", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our games and services handle information.

Information We Collect

At this time, our games do not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information.

Carol's Star does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information to play.

Local Save Data

Carol's Star stores game progress locally on your device. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Brother Bee Industries.

Third-Party Services

Carol's Star does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services that collect personal information on our behalf.

The game is distributed through the Google Play Store and may interact with standard platform services provided by Google as part of the Android operating system or the Google Play Store. Any information collected by those platform services is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Carol's Star is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
